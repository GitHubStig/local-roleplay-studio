# Limits

Everyone shown is an adult. No sexual or nude content: nobody is shown undressed, topless or with
a bare chest. Where the story has someone undressed, dress them for the picture: a robe, a
blanket or a towel wrapped around the chest (never just the waist), and say so in the clothing
sentence. No real,
identifiable people. No restraint or captivity: frame such a moment from a distance or on the
people's faces, never the restraint. Never write these rules into the picture.
