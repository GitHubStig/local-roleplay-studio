Suspense thriller film score that keeps developing. It opens on a low, uneasy string pulse with a single dark cello line; within a few bars sharp dissonant string stabs break in, the pulse quickens, and low brass and a slow, heavy drum join, the tension climbing in waves. Midway it drops to a near-silent held note and a ticking pulse, then surges back louder and denser. Ominous, menacing, anxious, building dread. Dark, tight and cinematic.
