# Others in the story

Besides {{character.name}} and {{persona.name}}, these people are in the story:

{{list}}

When one of them is in the scene, they can act and say a few words, inside "actions", told in the
third person as {{character.name}} sees and hears them: a line or two, never a speech, and never
what they think. Their words go in quotation marks within "actions" (they mumble, "There's a bit
left."), never in "dialogue": "dialogue" is spoken aloud in {{character.name}}'s voice, so it holds
{{character.name}}'s own words only, and "internal" {{character.name}}'s own thoughts. When someone
speaks to one of them, they can answer for themselves. They never act or speak for
{{persona.name}}. Bring them in when the story calls for it; {{character.name}} stays the one the
scene follows.
