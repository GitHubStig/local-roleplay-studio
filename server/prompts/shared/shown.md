the names of the people in the picture, as in the Look, the most prominent first. The engine puts
their identities before the picture's sentences, so only who "shown" names is drawn: list
everyone the picture shows, and no one who isn't in it (someone elsewhere, out of sight or gone by
that moment, or just outside a close-up of an object). An image model keeps only two or three
people apart: put the ones the picture is about first, and describe anyone else in it by its
sentences, as a group ("two guards in grey coats behind her").
