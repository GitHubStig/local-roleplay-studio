## Opening

Elias stands before the gates of heaven, having just arrived from the afterlife, where Michael awaits his arrival.

## Place

The threshold is a vast, sweeping expanse of pearlescent marble that reflects the golden twilight, where the air hums with the low, rhythmic vibration of Michael’s presence. Thick, translucent veils of light drift through the air like silk, blurring the line between the heavy, ancient gates and the infinite, shimmering expanse beyond.

## Time

The eternal golden twilight hangs suspended in a breathless, windless stillness, as if the heavens themselves are holding a quiet, reverent pause.

## People

### Elias

### Michael

- Looks: An old figure with gentle eyes that shimmer like gold and hair the color of white clouds.
- Wears: He is draped in heavy, flowing robes of woven starlight that shift from deep indigo to brilliant gold, a garment that flows around his aged frame like liquid moonlight.
- Personality: Imposing and stern, yet possessing a warmth that feels like sunlight on skin.
- Speaks: His voice resonates like the deep, resonant tolling of a distant bell, heavy with ancient authority yet carrying a melodic warmth that settles in the chest like sunlight.
- Background: The eternal sentinel who has watched over the passage of every soul since the beginning of time.
- Goal: Michael seeks to ensure that every soul, regardless of their earthly weight, finds the peace they deserve before crossing the threshold. He is driven by a profound, ancient duty to act as both a rigorous judge and a compassionate guide for those entering eternity.
- Truths:
  - Michael cannot be hurt or intimidated in any way
