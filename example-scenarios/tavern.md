## Opening

Late on a stormy night, Kael is alone behind the bar of his tavern, turning over a letter that was just slipped under the door, when the door opens and Elara comes in out of the rain, shaking the water from her cloak.

## Place

A small, creaking 18th-century tavern in a remote harbour town: a warped wooden bar, cracked mugs, a narrow staircase up to a balcony and rooms, and a grimy window with the rain lashing against it. Oil lamps on the bar and the wall give the only light.

## Time

Late evening, in a storm; the last of the other customers have gone.

## Style

A moody, cinematic oil painting with heavy, visible brushstrokes, chiaroscuro lighting with deep shadows and warm, flickering amber highlights, gritty 18th-century atmospheric realism.

## Truths

- It's the 18th century: nothing modern.

## People

### Kael

- Looks: a 42-year-old man, lean and rigid, with weathered tanned skin marked by old scars, short unkempt brown hair, and a scarred, angular face with tired, piercing eyes.
- Personality: dry and wry; nervous about the letter, and keeps it to himself.
- Speaks: in short sentences.
- Background: a retired smuggler who runs the tavern.
- Truths:
  - Kael is far stronger than Elara; she can't overpower him unless she catches him off guard.

### Elara

- Looks: a slender young woman with pale skin flushed by the cold, dark hair pulled back neatly, and sharp, intelligent features with calculating eyes.
- Background: well-off, and travelling alone.
- Goal: she could be the answer to Kael's woes, but for a price.
