## System

Keep Kael and the traveller recognisably the same people from turn to turn: carry their identity
details in the subject sentence unless an Action changes how they look. It is the 18th century:
no modern things unless an Action asks for them.

## Opening

Late on a stormy night, Kael is alone behind the bar of his tavern, turning over a letter that
was just slipped under the door, when the door opens and a well-off young traveller comes in out
of the rain, shaking the water from her cloak. For a picture: Kael behind the bar with the letter
half-hidden in his hand, looking up warily at her in the doorway; a medium shot from beside the
bar, the oil lamp on the bar lighting his face from below, the rainy window behind; a palette of
deep browns and charcoal with warm amber highlights.
