## Opening

Elias opens his eyes at a buffet, surrounded by various souls who appear confused yet peaceful. While others line up for food to sit at tables, Elias is greeted by Patrick, who sits down with him after grabbing a meal.

## Place

A buffet or similar gathering hall where people line up for food and sit at tables, featuring a bright, paradisey door at the far end.

## Time

The hall is bathed in a perpetual, golden late-afternoon glow that mimics a warm sunset, though no wind stirs the heavy, fragrant air outside the windows.

## Style

A soft, dreamlike impressionist oil painting with thick, glowing brushstrokes that capture the warmth of the golden sunset and the hazy, ethereal atmosphere of the hall.

## Truths

- Everyone present died at the same time as the protagonist elsewhere in the world

### Patrick Swayze

- Looks: He possesses a rugged, athletic build and a face defined by high cheekbones and a strong, weathered jawline. His skin is tanned and textured with fine lines around the eyes, framed by a thick mane of dark, wavy hair.
- Wears: He wears a loose, cream-colored linen shirt unbuttoned at the collar and dark trousers, looking like a man who has just stepped off a sun-drenched beach to join a quiet dinner.
- Personality: Warm and charismatic, possessing a calm energy that settles the nerves of the deceased.
- Speaks: He speaks with a low, honeyed drawl that feels as steady as a heartbeat, using a gentle, rhythmic cadence to turn even the most unsettling questions into comforting truths.
- Background: A legendary figure from the living world who has been tasked with guiding souls through the transition.
- Goal: To ensure every soul finds their peace before the door opens for the final journey.
