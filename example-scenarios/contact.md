## Opening

The jungle is completely still, until Mac screams "Contact!" straight at the camera, face twisted with rage, veins standing out on his neck. He swings the minigun up, its barrels spinning into a blur; the first muzzle flash lights up the dark undergrowth, and spent brass pours around his boots in a golden stream.

The rest of the squad runs in beside him and forms a tight firing line, shoulder to shoulder: Dutch firing his rifle and grenade launcher, Dillon both submachine guns, Poncho lobbing explosive rounds, Billy firing beside them. Close up: chattering bolts, flying brass, sweaty faces shouting over the noise.

The jungle in front of them is torn apart: fronds shredded to pulp, trunks bursting into clouds of splinters and sawdust, heavy branches crashing down. White smoke and falling wood fill the air as the men step forward, shaking with the recoil.

Then Mac's minigun runs dry and the barrels wind down, clicking. Dutch lowers his rifle and raises a hand, and one by one the others stop firing. In the silence there's only ragged breathing and hissing steam. Last, a slow pan across the clearing: where the jungle stood is a smoking, hollow waste of shattered wood.

## Place

A dense, claustrophobic jungle: a thick canopy, hanging vines, broad green fronds and tall trunks close on every side, the air thick with humidity.

## Time

Daytime, the light under the canopy dim and green.

## Style

A gritty 1980s action film still on 35mm, with heavy grain, high contrast and a sweaty, humid haze.
