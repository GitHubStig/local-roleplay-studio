# Expand on the Scenario page (2026-11-03, Mac)

What the Scenario page's Expand call ([ADR 0032](../adr/0032-scenarios-are-stories-kept-in-each-session.md), `server/scenario/expand.ts`) does on Gemma 4 26B Heretic (nvfp4, Ollama, Thinking off), run through the app's API on the example Scenarios and on one typed Brief: "A lighthouse keeper in a winter storm; I wash up at her door, half drowned, with a sealed box she mustn't open. Watercolour."

- **A Scenario was copied out in full and ran past the 2048-token cap** when the schema asked for every part, though the code keeps only what it adds. So a Scenario's schema now asks only for who plays whom, the suggestions, and the People's names when it lists none. Then the tavern and Contact took 4–5 s each.
- **People came back as titles** ("The Keeper", "The Drowned Guest") from the Brief until the schema's name field said "a first name, or first and last; never a title", as the Cast's does. Then: Elias and Clara, or Elara and Kael (the tavern's names, though the call doesn't see the tavern).
- **Asked for anyone else in the Opening's scene, it added people who aren't there** (Silas the Harbor Master and Bram the Deckhand, or Thomas, a constable, to the tavern, in two of three runs), wording against it ("someone only mentioned … is the story's to bring in") or not. So the People of a Scenario that lists them are kept as they are, and the model names them only for a Scenario that lists none.
- **Whitespace loops until the cap**: 1 seed in 6 on the Brief and 2 in 8 on the tavern (the call sent straight to Ollama, seeds 1–8), each reply 99% whitespace and 32 s long. Always right after `"character"`: the model took its answer as done, but the schema still required `suggestions`, so `}` was barred and it padded, the Storyboard plan's trap ([people-in-pictures.md](people-in-pictures.md)). Making `suggestions` optional stopped the loops but it then suggested nothing (0 in 8); putting it first made them worse (5 in 8). What fixed it, as for the plan: the user message names the reply's parts in order and says it's done only when the suggestions are written. Then none in 32 (the tavern at seeds 1–16, Contact and the Brief at 1–8), with 3–10 suggestions each on the tavern, where there had been none, 4–6 on Contact and 6–10 on the Brief, in 3–11 s. A failed reply is still retried once with a new seed (`withRetry`).
- **From the Brief**, both runs at 12–13 s: the Watercolour style in Style, "The box must not be opened" as a Truth, a lighthouse in a storm as the Place, the Brief's "I" as the player, and nine or ten suggestions (goals, backgrounds, looks, Truths), each specific to the story.
- **From the tavern** (stated almost in full), no suggestions in two runs before that fix, though Elara's personality, way of speaking and goal are blank. From Contact, one, written as "You believe…", and no player or character picked, which leaves them to the Cast.

A Roleplay started through the API from the page's tavern, with the roles swapped and Elara's goal typed in, got a Cast with Elara as the Character pursuing that goal and Kael as the Persona, both Truths kept.

**Suggest for one part** (`server/scenario/suggest.ts`), added because Expand suggests for only some blanks: on the tavern, 0.4–0.7 s a suggestion (Elara's personality, way of speaking, goal and a Truth about her; a story Truth), each fitting what's stated; asked twice for Elara's goal, it gave two different goals.

With what the player typed as notes (2026-11-03, Mac), Suggest builds on it: "revenge on the harbourmaster" for Elara's goal came back as a fuller goal about the harbourmaster's corruption, in about a second.
