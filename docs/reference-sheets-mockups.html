For reference-sheets-plans.md, as decided on 2026-11-25: a Settings section and a switch when starting; every Subject's sheet ticked, made in the Look while the player reviews (a Roleplay's Cast review, a Storyboard's plan review), with Redo and Change…, all settled once the story begins; the picture's chip; the Session manager's new part. Where a choice is still open, the first option is the one I'd pick. Drawn with the tavern's Kael and Elara.
