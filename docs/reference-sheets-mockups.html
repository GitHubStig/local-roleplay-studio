Mockups for reference-sheets-plans.md. Each section has options; the first is the one I'd pick. Drawn with the tavern's Kael and Elara and the sheets from the 2026-11-25 bench; a Roleplay's screens, as a Storyboard's plan review works the same way (section 6).

1. Turning it on

Settings say whether new Sessions start with sheets; each Session can turn them on or off for itself, as it can Render replies.

3. Redo and change a sheet

The sheet holds for the whole Session, so it's worth getting right. A change goes through the identity, so the words and the sheet always agree.

A · Change… asks what's different ✓ Picked
Change Kael's sheet
fewer scars, and grey at the temples
His identity becomes:
a 42-year-old man with a lean, rigid build, weathered tanned skin with a single old scar on his cheek, short unkempt brown hair greying at the temples…
CancelMake the sheet

The Text Model rewrites the identity from the note, shown before the sheet is made, so the player sees what will change in every Frame's prompt too.

Three renders for the price of one choice (about 4 min on the Mac, 1 on the PC), at the start, when the player is reviewing anyway. Could be just the first time, with Redo making one after.
