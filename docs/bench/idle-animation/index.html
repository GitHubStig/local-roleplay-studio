Idle animation: blink and lamp flicker

Kael tavern, Frame 9 (Qwen-Image 2.1). Elara's half-closed and closed eyes are masked edits, pasted into the same picture; everything else is the original pixel for pixel. The flicker is drawn live in the browser: no rendering.

Elara, enlarged
Blink

Every 2–6 s at random, now and then twice. A blink lasts about 120–150 ms.

Lamp flicker
Lantern flame

Wobble sways the flame's own pixels, the tip most. Painted shapes fade slowly between differently shaped flames, each an edit of just the flame.

Flat: a warm glow falling off with distance across the picture. Depth: falling off with distance in 3D from SHARP's depth, brighter on what faces the flame, so Elara and the bar catch the lantern and Kael, far from it, doesn't. Painted: fades between the picture and an edit with the flame flaring up, inside the lantern's mask. All waver on layered noise, with quick dips; the room dims a little when they do. Shadows don't move.
