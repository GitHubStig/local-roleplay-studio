# Showcase

Material for a public page about Local Roleplay Studio, collected as it happens, for the page to be built from later, in this folder (as [retrospectives/screens-redesign/](../retrospectives/screens-redesign/index.html) is). It's the owner's own project, not a feature of the app: after two months of building every day, a lot was built, and as much was tried and not built, which is why `docs/bench/`, `docs/research/` and a few artifacts are kept.

## What the page is for

Three things, the last as important as the others:

1. **What the app is.** It began as a way to write Image Prompts with a local Text Model, change them and see each one rendered by a local Image Model, with no other apps and no cloud services. It grew into one app that brings text, image, voice, video, music and 2.5D/3D models together in the form of a game: a roleplay or companion chat (a Roleplay), or a story planned ahead (a Storyboard) or steered picture by picture (a Chain), pictured, voiced, scored and animated as it's played. Plenty of tools wrap ComfyUI and other runtimes in workflows, and this one does too, but as a story to play rather than a workflow to run. It's local first, and it's also a testbed for AI models of every kind: when a new one comes out, it can be benchmarked here, or added to the app, which is what `docs/bench/` and `docs/research/` record.
2. **What was built, and how we got there**: the benches, retrospectives, ADRs and research behind each part.
3. **What was tried and dropped, and why**: the experiments that didn't generalise, didn't scale or were overtaken by something better.

## How it started

The owner's own account, moved from [design.md](../design.md) on 2026-11-22, as it was written:

> The original aim was to make it easy to iterate on Image Prompts and test them across image-generation models. I found [Draw Things](https://drawthings.ai) and [ComfyUI](https://www.comfy.org) hard to learn and overwhelming when all I wanted was to iterate on prompts; discovering [mflux](https://github.com/mflux-community/mflux) and Qwen-Image 2.1 led me to a simpler, more playful interface. Chain began as one-step-at-a-time prompt editing; Storyboard grew from wanting to build a short comic more quickly, and Roleplay added prompt-making to an ongoing character conversation.

## Built, and how we got there

A line each: the part, and what tells its story.

- **The screens.** Redesigned in November: [retrospectives/screens-redesign/](../retrospectives/screens-redesign/index.html), with before and after shots, and [ADR 0039](../adr/0039-every-session-screen-has-one-shape.md).
- **3D from a picture.** A Frame's picture as a 2.5D scene (SHARP), and a person as a whole 3D figure (TripoSplat, LiTo): the survey in [research/image-to-3d.md](../research/image-to-3d.md). Something to see is the point here: a small hosted interactive scene if practical, otherwise a screenshot or short capture. (From open threads, the owner, 2026-10-26.)
- **The camera moved by an edit.** [bench/camera/](../bench/camera/README.md): a re-render can't keep the people and place while the camera moves, an edit can; decided in [ADR 0038](../adr/0038-the-camera-is-moved-by-an-edit.md).
- **People in a Roleplay.** Who's in the picture and what they wear, carried from Frame to Frame: [research/people-in-pictures.md](../research/people-in-pictures.md), [bench/two-people/](../bench/two-people/README.md); Truths against a player who overpowers everyone, [bench/truths/](../bench/truths/README.md); others in the scene, [bench/others/](../bench/others/README.md).
- **Music that follows the story.** [bench/music-models/](../bench/music-models/README.md) (MiniMax Music 3 against ACE-Step 1.5, by ear), [bench/music-director/](../bench/music-director/README.md) (where the music changes), [bench/music-cues/](../bench/music-cues/README.md) (what each Cue asks for); [research/music-in-play.md](../research/music-in-play.md) for what's still hard.
- **Clips.** Each kind of clip, across LTX 2.5, Wan 2.2 and MiniMax H3: [bench/video-ltx/](../bench/video-ltx/README.md), [bench/video-kinds/](../bench/video-kinds/README.md), [research/video-models.md](../research/video-models.md).
- **Voices.** [bench/voices/](../bench/voices/README.md) and [research/voices.md](../research/voices.md). A line that never ended (2026-11-24): found in an upgrade check as 82 s of buzz, narrowed to two tag combinations, one fixed in the tags and both caught by a cap on a line's frames set from 201 bench lines, with one retry ([research/voices.md](../research/voices.md#lines-that-dont-end-2026-11-24-mac), [bench/voices/cap/](../bench/voices/cap/README.md)).
- **The Session manager.** Keeping Sessions, sized by part, exported and imported as zips, trimmed in place (2026-11-24): [design/session-manager.md](../design/session-manager.md), [ADR 0043](../adr/0043-session-export-and-import.md). Planned in one long grilling and built in four stages; the bench that picked its compression found deflating a Session's pictures and clips saved 0–4% for 20 to 60 times the time, so they're stored ([bench/session-zip/](../bench/session-zip/README.md)). Checked on the Windows PC the next day, where deflate ran 4 to 6 times slower: its 3D, deflated until then for 7%, made a Session of SHARP scenes zip at 15 MB/s, so 3D is stored too and zips 20 times faster; the same check found the folder dialog opening behind the browser.
- **Clone, in place of forking a Session** (2026-11-25). Forking a Session had been on the owner's mind for months; Clone on the Session manager makes a new Session as exporting and importing it would, from what's ticked, so trying another way from a point in a story is clone it and carry on in the copy, with nothing new in a Session's data: [design/session-manager.md](../design/session-manager.md).
- **The app on a phone.** Every screen driven on the Mac's iPhone simulator with real touches through XCUITest (2026-11-25), and what it found fixed: the keyboard over the text box, sound that iOS wouldn't start, music playing on in a tab left behind, landscape, the 3D viewer by touch: [research/phone.md](../research/phone.md).
- **Image Models.** How a picture forms and how many steps it needs, [bench/steps/](../bench/steps/README.md); precision and memory, [bench/precision/](../bench/precision/README.md); [research/image-models.md](../research/image-models.md).
- **A Roleplay's Look in the Cast review** (2026-11-25). The identities and style every picture shares were written lazily at the first picture; now a job queued as the Cast is saved writes them while the player reads it (2.2 s on the Mac), so they can be edited before anything is pictured, and reference sheets can be made from them before the story begins. A hand edit to how the scene starts asks whether the Look follows, rather than throwing the player's edits away: [ADR 0044](../adr/0044-a-roleplays-look-is-written-in-the-cast-review.md), [design/roleplay.md](../design/roleplay.md).
- **The face pass after an edit** (2026-11-26, reference sheets' stage 5). An edit of a picture in a Session with sheets is followed by a face pass in the same job, only for the heads its painted area touches (all of them for a camera move). As first built it undid edits of a face, a smile put back and a cap and a cut painted out on every seed, as stage 0 had found the pass paints out whatever it isn't told to keep; naming the edit's own instruction as the change to keep kept all three, while a camera move stays told the Frame's expression, which held a close-up's gaze better. [bench/reference-sheets/2026-11-26/edits/](../bench/reference-sheets/2026-11-26/edits/README.md).
- **The face edit's reference cache** (2026-11-26, after stage 6). The open thread called it a step cache that might change the faces; the records showed it keeps the references' attention between steps and draws the same picture, so the bench only had to confirm it through the face pass: ~53 s to ~31 s a face on the PC, the faces the same. The owner then asked for the Mac's run: mflux's edit had that cache already, but its real step cache halved the Mac's face edit too (~210 s to ~105 s), the faces nearly the same. A picture with sheets is now about the 3× Settings had been saying: [bench/reference-sheets/2026-11-26/cache/](../bench/reference-sheets/2026-11-26/cache/README.md).
- **The Turbo edit's cache at `gpu`, dropped** (2026-11-26). The base edit's cache at `gpu` had saved ~40% of a face edit, so the Turbo's, at the template's `auto`, was benched the same way: no gain, as its 8 steps are ~3 s of an ~18 s edit, the rest loading and encoding, and the pictures the same. Left at `auto`: [bench/reference-sheets/2026-11-26/cache/](../bench/reference-sheets/2026-11-26/cache/README.md#the-turbo-edits-cache-at-gpu-2026-11-26-the-pc).
- **A ComfyUI job after a sleep** (2026-11-26). A bench had waited for good after the Mac slept, and reading the ComfyUI client showed the app's jobs would too: a WebSocket left half-open hears nothing, and ComfyUI still answers. Each check now also asks ComfyUI's queue whether the job's prompt is still there. The first try called every real job lost, as a finished prompt leaves the queue 4.5 s before ComfyUI says it's done, so it waits four checks; then a proxy that froze the socket mid-edit showed the job fail a minute after: [research/comfyui.md](../research/comfyui.md#a-job-after-this-computer-sleeps-2026-11-26).
- **Marks on a face, and an earlier edit's change** (2026-11-26). The face pass painted out a render's bruises and an earlier edit's cap or cut. Three ways that didn't work, each dropped: a general sentence (keep whatever is on the skin), which changed nothing; naming every earlier edit, which painted back what a later edit had let go (a faded cut, a smile); and writing marks in the expression sentence, which lost them along a story, as the clothing sentence is what carries them from Frame to Frame. What was built: an edit made from faces already the sheets' is repainted lighter (0.9), and a Roleplay's pass names the Frame's clothing sentence, where the bruise was written: [bench/reference-sheets/2026-11-26/marks/](../bench/reference-sheets/2026-11-26/marks/README.md).
- **A thing's sheet in the face pass** (2026-11-26). The face pass, told "the face and hair" of a lantern, painted a woman's face into its glass. Told to take its sheet's shape and keep how it's lit instead, it took the sheet every time with no face. The app learns a thing from the sheet's own check (no head on it), kept in `session.json` v6 with a Storyboard Frame's clothing sentence: [bench/reference-sheets/2026-11-26/object/](../bench/reference-sheets/2026-11-26/object/README.md).
- **How hair is worn, in the Look** (2026-11-26, after stage 6). Asked to fix the hairstyle of someone a Scenario leaves vague, the bench found the Look had been naming no hair at all, even where the Scenario stated it: the Cast summed people up without it, and the Look's writer saw only the Cast. Given the Scenario's facts as a Storyboard's plan is, and asked how hair is worn, it kept the stated hair word for word and picked one where none was given, on most seeds. Two other wordings that seemed clearer were worse: [bench/reference-sheets/2026-11-26/hair/](../bench/reference-sheets/2026-11-26/hair/README.md).
- **The name in front of each identity** (2026-11-26). A Roleplay played on moonlight-dusk-uncensored 26b had pictures whose prompts described its people without naming them: asked to start each identity with the name, which the Look's reply already gives in a field of its own, that model never did (0 of 24), where Gemma 4 26B and Qwen3.8 27B always did. So the identity now leaves the name out and the engine puts it in front wherever it's used (`server/identity.ts`), and the stored Sessions were rewritten once by hand, not migrated. [Bench](../bench/identity-name/README.md).
- **Sheets for a Session started without them** (2026-11-26, after stage 6). Sheets were only for a Session started with them; the idea was to offer them at a Roleplay's Restart. Built instead as a switch at the top of the Look, for as long as the story hasn't begun, so any Storyboard or Roleplay still in its review can have them, restarted or not: [research](../research/people-in-pictures.md#done-since-the-build-2026-11-26).
- **"Make him smile", told its change** (2026-11-26, after stage 6). An edit of the whole picture was always told like a camera move, so the face pass put a smile the player asked for back to the Frame's expression. The Camera list's wording is now marked as a move (`queue.json`'s first migration since versions began), and any other edit is told its change: the smile kept, through the app. It showed a later edit's pass fading an earlier edit's cut: [bench/reference-sheets/2026-11-26/app/](../bench/reference-sheets/2026-11-26/app/README.md#make-him-smile-with-nothing-painted).
- **Start's menu and the face pass's files** (2026-11-26, after stage 6). Start's menu offered sheets on a ComfyUI that couldn't run the face pass; it now asks that ComfyUI, as Settings does, and greys the switch saying why. Settings' own message, its file patterns and all, overran the menu, so the menu says only where to look: [research](../research/people-in-pictures.md#done-since-the-build-2026-11-26).
- **Reference sheets through the app** (2026-11-26, after stage 6). What the stages had checked only by the tests, run through the app: a four-Frame Storyboard with sheets, its face pass and two edits on the PC, and sheets made and checked on the Mac's mflux. No bug found; a guarded gaze softened by the pass, as before. A replay for the Storyboard was kept: [bench/reference-sheets/2026-11-26/app/](../bench/reference-sheets/2026-11-26/app/README.md).
- **Reference sheets, finished** (2026-11-26, stage 6). The feature is done: [ADR 0045](../adr/0045-faces-are-repainted-from-a-reference-sheet.md). Each sheet is now checked before it's used, by the same Qwen3-VL. Across 42 sheets by seven Image Models, the face pass's own question couldn't count a sheet's views: it answered the sheet's thirds, and on Krea 2's only one. Asking how many times the Subject is shown, with the head count stage 4 had dropped saying person or thing, caught every unfit sheet by eye and no fit one. FIBO 2.0 Turbo wrote names on 2 sheets of 6; Anima Turbo drew views out of order, two alike or four lanterns on 3. A bench of the check on the Mac first sent two of its questions empty, a shell slip, not the model. The pickers now say which models drew unfit sheets: [bench/reference-sheets/2026-11-26/check/](../bench/reference-sheets/2026-11-26/check/README.md).

## Tried and dropped

A line each: what it was, why it went, and what took its place.

- **Idle animation** (2026-10-06 to 10-29). Making a still picture feel alive between Frames: a blink, lamp light, a flickering flame. It worked on the demos but didn't generalise to every picture or scale, and image-to-video, the Idle Loop clip, did it easily once built. [bench/idle-animation/](../bench/idle-animation/README.md), [ADR 0025](../adr/0025-clips-by-kind.md).
- **Tags instead of prose for the Art Agent** (2026-09-29 to 10-26). A few tags per aspect of a picture, for speed or tag-trained models; prose did better on every model measured, as tags can't say who does what to whom. [bench/art-tags/](../bench/art-tags/README.md), [ADR 0023](../adr/0023-the-art-agent-writes-prose.md).
- **JSON captions for the models trained on them** (2026-10-11 to 10-27). Not built: the models that read them read prose as well. [ADR 0033](../adr/0033-no-json-captions-from-the-art-agent.md).
- **A figure inside its 3D scene** (2026-10-04, decided 10-28). SHARP's room with the TripoSplat or LiTo figure placed where the person was: it worked as an image, but not well enough to ship. [ADR 0024](../adr/0024-no-figure-in-its-room.md).
- **Pictures that agree, for clips between them** (dropped 2026-11-01). Each Roleplay picture shows the room differently, which LTX 2.5 couldn't join; Wan 2.2 and MiniMax H3 could, so it wasn't needed. [research/video-models.md](../research/video-models.md#into-next-between-pictures-that-dont-agree-an-ltx-problem-the-owner-2026-11-01).
- **MiniMax Music 3** (2026-11-07 to 08). Benched against ACE-Step 1.5 by ear, and dropped: its tracks were its own length, and voices came in where none were asked. [ADR 0035](../adr/0035-music-from-ace-step-through-comfyui.md).
- **Posing a Character with a ControlNet** (2026-10-09 to 11-22). Posing a figure for a Frame: more work than a player would put in, when an edit model fixes a pose from a sentence. Its suggested uses are in [research/people-in-pictures.md](../research/people-in-pictures.md#controlnets-suggested-uses-untried).
- **Thumbnails as a file per picture.** Dropped for the files they add; a sprite sheet is the idea now (open threads).
- **A Frame all or nothing** ([ADR 0003](../adr/0003-turns-are-all-or-nothing.md), replaced by [ADR 0030](../adr/0030-a-chain-frame-renders-as-a-job.md)), and **a Storyboard in one call** ([ADR 0006](../adr/0006-storyboards-plan-in-one-call.md), replaced by [ADR 0019](../adr/0019-storyboards-plan-review-write.md)): designs replaced as the app grew.

## Tried, not built yet

- **Reference portraits** (2026-10-06 on the Mac; a day of trials on 2026-11-22, both machines). A portrait of each person to keep faces the same: in the render it holds faces but pulls expressions and poses; repainting only the faces after the render keeps the moment, with the faces found by the vision model already loaded. The collages, full-size crops, and four conclusions that turned out wrong, three overturned by the owner's questions (a ComfyUI "regression" that was a seed, "one face per edit" that was wording, "prompt order" that was a prompt with no looks in it) and one by a closer look (a "dark band" that was a seam): [bench/reference-portraits/2026-11-22/](../bench/reference-portraits/2026-11-22/README.md). On the way it found a real bug, edits reusing their picture's seed, fixed the same day. A follow-up (2026-11-25) found the references can come from any model, even as photographs, as the edit repaints the face in the picture's own style, and that a character sheet (one render, three angles), the owner's idea, stops a portrait's pose turning the face: [bench/reference-portraits/2026-11-25/](../bench/reference-portraits/2026-11-25/README.md). Then the plan to build it (in git history; its record is now in [research](../research/people-in-pictures.md#reference-sheets-as-built-2026-11-26)), with mockups the owner picked from, and its first stage of benches: the face pass must name the expression it keeps, a count of the heads catches a close-up that pasted the sheet in, and Qwen3-VL beat four detectors at finding heads, as the only one that says whose a head is: [bench/reference-sheets/2026-11-25/](../bench/reference-sheets/2026-11-25/README.md). The sheets were then made from the Look by the server and put on screen (2026-11-26): a Settings section, a switch in Start's menu, and each Subject's sheet under its identity while the story is set up, with Redo and Change…, which shows the rewritten identity before the sheet is made from it, as the owner picked from the mockups. Then the face pass after each render, through the app (2026-11-26): it made Kael's head bigger and turned him from Elara to the viewer, as the owner spotted; a tighter area and an instruction about size changed nothing, seed for seed; one view of the sheet in place of the whole gave Elara her sheet's face at last; and starting the repaint part-way from the render (0.96) kept Kael's turn. On the way the real runs found two bugs the tests then covered, and a bench that slept with the laptop and was said to be running: [bench/reference-sheets/2026-11-26/](../bench/reference-sheets/2026-11-26/README.md). Last, the check the plan was written for: the owner's Princess Roleplay built again with and without sheets, her side buns drifting in only by the fourth Frame without, there from the first with her sheet steered to them, her face and her captor's the same throughout; the face pass painted out her bruises, a thing to name next (the plan's stage 4).
