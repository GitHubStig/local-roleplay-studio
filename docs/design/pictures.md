# Pictures

Part of the [design](../design.md). Terms in **bold** are defined in [CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md).

- **Seed:** fixed for the whole Session: Settings' fixed seed, or a random one picked when it starts. Everything the Session makes is seeded from it ([ADR 0011](../adr/0011-one-seed-per-session.md)): pictures and upscales with the seed itself; each spoken line with the seed plus its Frame's index; each take of the voice with the seed plus how many came before; and each Text Model call with the seed plus a hash of what's asked plus how many times the Session asked it before (`server/text/seeded.ts`). So a fixed-seed Session played the same way asks with the same seeds, while a retry or a new take gets a new one. 3D figures use seed 42; SHARP samples nothing.
- **A Session keeps what defines its pictures** ([ADR 0015](../adr/0015-a-picture-per-image-model.md)): its Image Model, steps and seed, copied from Settings when it starts. The Image Model is switched in the Session, which resets the steps to the new model's default; a turbo with its own weights or LoRA is an Image Model of its own ([ADR 0016](../adr/0016-turbos-are-image-models.md)). Where it renders and how (size, step cache, quantize, float16, the Backends) is read from Settings at each render (ADR 0022), so a change there reaches running Sessions too; a Session may prefer a kind of backend (`imageBackend`). A model of the same name is the same model on either backend.
- **A Frame keeps a picture per Image Model** (`pictures`, `server/pictures.ts`), each with its upscale, edits, 3D, clips and stale flag. It shows the Session's model's picture, or else its latest, marked as by another model; a picture whose Image Prompt changed since is stale. A render replaces only its own model's picture, so switching back shows the old ones without rendering.
- **Upscale** enlarges a picture to 2048 px on its shortest edge with SeedVR2 (7B or 3B, as Settings say), on mflux or ComfyUI. The screens show the upscale where there is one. Upscales and 3D are made from the picture shown when the job starts.
- **Edits** ([ADR 0021](../adr/0021-picture-edits-are-versions.md), `server/pictureEdits.ts`): an `edit` job repaints a picture as told, in an area painted over it or all of it, with the edit model Settings or the Edit dialog choose (`server/images/editModels.ts`); on mflux, quantized as Settings say (`editQuantize`). Each edit is a version with its own image, instruction, seed and painted area, made from the newest by the same edit model, so each edit model's edits are a line of their own, its first made from the render. `picked` says which version is the picture; that one is shown, upscaled, made into 3D, compared and copied into a Chain. Only a line's newest edit can be made again or deleted. A render by the same model replaces the picture with its edits. **Moving the camera** is an edit too: the dialog's Camera list (`web/src/cameraMoves.ts`: low, high and overhead angles; close-ups on a face, eyes, hands, legs or feet; over the shoulder, from behind) writes a move into the instruction, worded as the camera bench found each kind works (a close-up as what the result shows; an angle or a viewpoint as the move and a line keeping the rest), speaking of "the woman" or "the man" for the player to change, as an edit model knows no names ([ADR 0038](../adr/0038-the-camera-is-moved-by-an-edit.md)). The Image Prompt stays as it was, as for any edit.
- **Clips** ([ADR 0025](../adr/0025-clips-by-kind.md), [ADR 0026](../adr/0026-clips-per-video-model.md)): short videos made from a picture by kind (Lead In, Idle Loop, Play out, Into Next), on a ComfyUI, by a video model (LTX 2.5, Wan 2.2 or MiniMax H3, picked in the Clip section, starting on Settings' default), their prompt written by the Text Model from the story and editable. A picture keeps a clip of each kind per video model, and a kind made by two or more can be played side by side, in step (Compare). A clip belongs to the picture by the Session's Image Model; a picture by another model has none until that model renders the Frame. Each video model makes clips at the size Settings give it, Small (~0.4 MP) or Large (its trained size, ~0.9 MP), else its own (LTX Large, Wan and H3 Small), and offers only the lengths it fits at that size on a 12 GB card ([ADR 0027](../adr/0027-a-clip-size-per-video-model.md)).
- **3D (experimental)**, made from a picture: **SHARP** (Apple; `server/3d/scene.ts`) turns the whole picture into a 2.5D scene of Gaussian splats that turns about 30°, inventing more the further it turns; **TripoSplat** (VAST; `server/3d/figure.ts`) cuts the person out and builds them whole as a figure that turns all the way round, best with one person unobstructed; **LiTo** (Apple, Mac only, [ADR 0028](../adr/0028-lito-on-the-mac-only.md); research-only weights) does the same as TripoSplat, kept apart. Each runs once per job as a uv script, so its memory is freed when it's done. Their limits are in [research/image-to-3d.md](../research/image-to-3d.md).
- **Compare** (a mode of All Frames, opened from the end of a Session's strip; ADR 0039): every pictured Frame's picture by each model side by side; with two models or more, an **Export** to a self-contained static page (`web/export.html`, pictures as WebP, written to `exports/<Session id>/`; one at a time per Session, a second refused while the first is written, as each starts by clearing the folder) that can optionally ask visitors to confirm they're 18 or older, a courtesy, not a lock. **Style scores** measure how varied each model's art style is across the Frames, from CSD and CLIP embeddings (`python/style/make.py`, kept in the Session's `style.json`); how and why: [research/style-scores.md](../research/style-scores.md), [ADR 0017](../adr/0017-no-vision-judge-for-style-scores.md). A hidden column's pictures can be deleted, unless it's the Session's own Image Model; that first cancels and removes the work on them: renders and clips by that model, and upscales, 3D and edits that would act on one of its pictures, whether the Frame shows it now or they're waiting on a render of it. It's refused while a style job is running, as that job is reading the pictures.
- **The thumbnail**: what shows a Session on Home is the picture chosen by its star (on hover, in All Frames and Compare), whichever Image Model made it, or else the newest Frame's picture; with no picture at all, a line of its text. A chosen one lasts until its file is deleted, and Home then goes back to the newest.

In testing, FLUX.2 Klein 4B keeps a person's face, hair and outfit consistent across Frames. For drift, see *reference images and edits for Subject consistency* in [open-threads.md](../open-threads.md).
