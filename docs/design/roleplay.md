# Roleplays

Part of the [design](../design.md). Terms in **bold** are defined in [CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md).

A Roleplay is its own module (`server/roleplay/`, `web/src/roleplay/`), with its own routes under `/api/sessions/:id/roleplay/` ([ADR 0007](../adr/0007-roleplay-is-a-conversation-with-a-cast.md)).

1. **The Cast:** one Text Model call writes the **Cast** (the Character, the Persona the player plays, the Setting) from the Brief, checked against every Limit. The player reviews it: edits it, or has it rewritten. A failed or cancelled first Cast discards the Session. A Scenario can start a Roleplay too: its Opening serves as the Brief, with what else it states, and who plays whom, as facts. The Scenario's Truths (the story's, then each Person's after their name, unless it starts with it) and its other People (`others`) are copied into the Cast, a box each, not written by the model, and stay editable; the others may act and say a few words inside the Character's actions, never in its spoken dialogue.
2. **Begin:** the Character's opening Reply, written under the same system message as every later Reply, becomes the Opening Frame.
3. **Each Message:** checked against every Limit; then, when the Cast has Truths, a judge call on the Text Model decides whether it breaks one and what happens instead, which the Character is told after the Message and the Frame keeps ([ADR 0032](../adr/0032-scenarios-are-stories-kept-in-each-session.md)); then the whole conversation goes to the Text Model: the system message (rules, Cast, reply format, Limits; the same every call), the opening Reply, each Message and Reply in turn, and the new Message. The Reply streams in field by field (`internal`, `actions`, `dialogue`) and is checked for sexual content. Either declined saves nothing and leaves the Message in the box. Replies are written with Ollama's `repeat_penalty` 1.15, which stopped long Roleplays looping on their own refrains.
4. **Undo** removes the latest exchange and puts its Message back; the opening can't be undone. With an earlier Frame selected it's **Undo to here**: once confirmed, every exchange after that Frame goes at once, with the Cues they began, and the first one's Message goes back. Once begun, only what steers the Character can be edited (personality, way of speaking, background, goal); how it started is fixed, and **Restart** takes the Roleplay back to reviewing its Cast, deleting every exchange and what was made from them, while the Cast, the Look and the voice stay.
5. **Suggest** writes a Message for the player, to edit or send (nothing is saved). The Text Model, Thinking off, reads the story as the Persona knows it (no thoughts), the Persona, and the player's last five Messages, and writes the next one as the player would type it: their capitalisation, person and length, only the Persona's own actions and words, and a move that takes the scene on. Anything already in the box is the player's steer. The suggestion is tidied and checked against the term list; it holds the Roleplay's lock while it writes.

**Picturing a Frame** (the Art Agent, by the Session's Text Model). Each new Reply is pictured as it arrives, while the Text Model is still loaded, so rendering later needs no reload between renders: the server queues its picture as it saves the Reply, after its spoken line and before its render when this browser's switches ask for them ([ADR 0031](../adr/0031-the-server-queues-what-follows-a-reply.md)); it's done with Pictures off too, so a Roleplay played without them can be rendered later. The first time, a call writes the Roleplay's **Look** from the Cast and Brief, the same shape as a Storyboard's: an identity for the Character, the Persona and anyone else the Brief names, and an art style fitted to the story's period. Then a call reads the story up to the Frame (thoughts left out, as a picture can't show them), writes the Frame's seven sentences as prose ([ADR 0023](../adr/0023-the-art-agent-writes-prose.md)), names anyone in the picture the Look doesn't have yet with their identity, and lists everyone in it, the most prominent first. Newcomers join the Look, so they look the same in every later picture. Clothing carries forward: the request gives each person's clothing sentence from the latest earlier picture showing them, or, for a Person not pictured yet, what the Scenario says they wear at the start; the Art Agent changes only what the story since changed, and dresses anyone whose clothes are given nowhere to suit the story (marks such as blood and dirt included), so a garment or a wound doesn't come and go between pictures. A picture that crosses a Limit is written once more, told which. With the Limits on, the Art Agent is told to dress anyone the story has undressed, and a picture of two or more people must name what each wears, or it's blocked and written again; with them off, those instructions and checks are skipped. Editing the Look rewrites every pictured Frame's Image Prompt, marking its pictures out of date.

**Chain from a Frame** ("Chain from Frame 3") on a pictured Frame makes a new Chain whose Opening Frame is that Frame alone: its Image Prompt as a Chain's nine sentences (the identities of everyone shown joined into one subject sentence, every word kept, as for a Storyboard's) and its pictures, copied with their upscales, 3D and picked edit but not their clips. The Brief, Settings and seed are the Roleplay's; the conversation, the other Frames and the Look stay behind, and the Roleplay is left as it is ([ADR 0018](../adr/0018-carry-on-in-a-new-session.md)). A Frame not pictured yet, or whose Image Prompt crosses a Limit, can't be.

**Voices** (`server/roleplay/voice.ts`; ADR 0010). The first time a line is spoken, the Text Model describes the Character's voice from the Cast in plain acoustic terms (`voice.md`), and the voice service designs a voice from it as a reference clip, saved with the Roleplay. Every line is then spoken by cloning that clip, so the Character sounds the same throughout; editing the description drops the clip, and the next line designs a new one. Only `dialogue` is spoken. Lines carry no emotion tags, which pulled the cloned voice off the Character (docs/research/voices.md); instead each line is directed: the Text Model reads the moment and picks a pace and a sound before it (a sigh, a laugh, a cough), which the voice service turns into Higgs's tags; if directing fails, the line is spoken as written, and says so. A Reply's thought can be spoken too, whispered, with a pace but never a sound. Designing and speaking are queued jobs that wait their turn with renders, so a voice and an image never compete for memory. On the Mac the voice service (`python/voice/serve.py`, mlx-audio) is started on first use, keeps one model loaded at a time and unloads it after a minute unused, and exits by itself if the server that started it is gone, so a restart never leaves it behind; on a PC, ComfyUI runs the same two models as workflows (`server/voice/comfyui/`), and the Text Model is unloaded before each voice when both are on that machine.
