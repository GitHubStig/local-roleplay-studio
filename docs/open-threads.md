- **Posing a Character with a ControlNet** (the owner's idea; first noted 2026-10-09 for mflux 0.22, as composition control). Prompts pose people badly: arms, hands, how someone sits. A ControlNet renders a picture to follow a control picture as well as its prompt; given a pose (an OpenPose skeleton, which a posed 3D figure can make), it directs the body and limbs and leaves the rest to the prompt. The idea: pose a figure (a small 3D mannequin in the app, or a pose picked from saved ones) and render the Frame to it. - **Reference portraits for Subject consistency** (rethought 2026-10-06 for mflux 0.21; split from picture edits 2026-10-22). The people in a Roleplay or Storyboard drift between Frames (a new seed, a new face), and the Look's words can only hold them so far. The idea: a portrait of each person, made once from the Look, passed with every render ("image 1 is Kael, image 2 is Elara Vance", then the Image Prompt). Each picture is still composed from its prompt, so the story leads and ADR 0005 holds (the Image Prompt is the state); the portraits only hold faces, hair and build steady. mflux 0.21's `mflux-generate-qwen-2.1-edit` runs Qwen-Image 2.1 with up to ten reference images, the references' attention cached across steps (`--use-kv-cache`, on by default); Viggle's turbo LoRA does it with 1–3 references in 6 steps. *Inputs must be RGB* (noted 2026-10-07): Qwen-Image 2.1's fourth channel is an edit mask, and a transparent input stays transparent, so a reference with alpha would come back see-through where it isn't opaque. ComfyUI's pictures were RGBA with alpha at 240–255 until 2026-10-07 (research/comfyui.md, "Alpha"), and older ones in Sessions still are: convert them to RGB first. The one deliberate exception is a cut-out on transparency (BiRefNet), e.g. a person alone as a reference. *Tried 2026-10-06* ([bench/reference-portraits](bench/reference-portraits/README.md)): portraits of Kael and Elara from the Kael tavern Roleplay's Look, then Frames 6, 9 and 12 without and with them. **The portraits hold identity well**: without them Elara is two different women in 9 and 12 and Kael's face changes in 12; with them both match their portraits in every Frame, and the two people stay apart. Costs: 1.4–1.7× the time (122–142 s against 84–86 s at 1024², Mac, some of it likely the edit tool's guidance), the same ~39 GB peak, the full 33 GB weights (the saved 8-bit copy can't take references), and the references pulling against the prompt (wider shots, smaller people; Frame 12 lost Elara's knives). Slots in behind `ImageGenerator`. *Another way, from mflux 0.23* (noted 2026-10-17): `mflux-generate-flux3-edit` runs FLUX.3 Klein (an Image Model since 2026-10-17) with up to ten reference images, in 16 steps, and takes `-q 8` (Qwen-Image 2.1's saved 8-bit copy can't take references). Not tried: whether its portraits hold identity as well as Qwen-Image 2.1's did, or its speed with references. Nor whether ComfyUI (the PC) can pass references with either model. *When someone's looks change* (noted 2026-10-22): a portrait holds a face against the prompt, so after "I turn into a demon" it would pull the picture back towards the old face. Here the whole check takes ~2 s, so the bridge would save about a second for a third-party fork: not worth it.
